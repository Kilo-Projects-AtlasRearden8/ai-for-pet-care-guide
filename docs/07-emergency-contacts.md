# Emergency Contacts and Plans

Hope for the best, prepare for the worst. One printed emergency page can save precious minutes if your pet gets sick or hurt.

## Why it matters

In an emergency it's hard to think clearly. Having phone numbers, addresses (on paper only), and a plan written down means anyone in the household can act fast.

## Step 1: Build your emergency contact list

Print this out and keep it on the fridge, near the phone, and in your pet's travel bag:

| Contact | Phone | When to call |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| Your veterinarian | [clinic phone] | Illness, injury, questions |
| Emergency vet clinic (nearest) | [clinic phone] | After-hours emergencies |
| Animal poison control | [poison control line] | Possible poisoning |
| Backup decision-maker (friend/family) | [phone] | If you can't be reached |
| Pet sitter or neighbor | [phone] | Help getting to the clinic |

Use example numbers only in any digital file. Write real numbers on the printed copy.

## Step 2: Ask AI to write your emergency plan

**Example prompt:**

> "Help me write a one-page pet emergency plan: what to do if my dog gets injured at home, who to call first, what to bring to the clinic, and how to safely transport a pet who is in pain. Keep it short, numbered, and easy to read in a hurry."

Review it carefully. Add anything specific to your home (carrier location, leash hook, nearest 24-hour clinic).

## Step 3: Know the signs of an emergency

Call a vet right away if your pet:

- Has trouble breathing
- Is unconscious or collapsing
- Has been hit by a vehicle or suffered serious injury
- Is bleeding heavily
- May have swallowed something poisonous
- Can't urinate or is straining to urinate

When in doubt, call. It's always better to be safe.

## Step 4: Practice the plan

Know where the carrier is. Know which door leads to the car fastest. Keep a spare leash by the door. A two-minute dry run makes a real emergency much calmer.

## Privacy reminder

Keep real names, phone numbers, and addresses on paper only — never in a public AI tool or shared digital file.
