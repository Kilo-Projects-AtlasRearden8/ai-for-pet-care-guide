## Who this is for

You don't need to be technical. If you can type a question into a search box, you can use the steps in this guide. It's written for:

- New pet owners figuring out routines
- Busy households sharing pet care between family members
- Pet sitters who need clear, written instructions
- Anyone who wants less mental load around pet care

## The golden rule: privacy first

**Never put your name, address, phone number, veterinary clinic details, or client information into a public AI tool.** Use nicknames for your pets ("the dog," "Milo") and general descriptions instead. Read the [privacy section](08-privacy-and-safety.md) before you start.
