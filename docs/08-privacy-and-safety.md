# Privacy and Safety

This is the most important section in this manual. Read it before you use any AI tool for pet care.

## The core rule

**Never share client or personal data with a public AI tool.**

That includes:

- Your full name, address, phone number, or email
- Your veterinarian's name, clinic name, or clinic address
- Your pet's microchip number
- Prescription numbers or pharmacy details
- Client information (if you care for other people's pets professionally)
- Credit card or payment information

## Use nicknames and general descriptions

Instead of:

> "My dog Luna at 123 Main Street takes heartworm medicine from Dr. Smith at Springfield Animal Clinic"

Try:

> "My dog takes heartworm medicine once a month. How do I set up a reminder schedule?"

The AI doesn't need your address to draft a care sheet.

## Prefer local AI when possible

If you can run AI on your own computer (a local model), do that for anything sensitive. Nothing leaves your device. This guide supports a local-first habit: try tools that run offline before tools that upload your words to a company's servers.

## Delete chats you don't need

Most AI tools keep a history of what you type. When you're done with a care sheet or medication draft, delete the conversation if the tool lets you. Less stored data means less risk.

## AI is not a veterinarian

AI can draft, summarize, and remind. It cannot:

- Examine your pet
- Diagnose illness
- Prescribe medication
- Tell you whether something is an emergency

Always confirm health decisions with a licensed veterinarian.

## If you're a pet sitter or professional caregiver

Treat your clients' information with the same care you'd want for your own. Never enter a client's name, address, pet details, or veterinary information into a public AI tool. Use generic placeholders in any drafts, then fill in real details by hand on printed copies.

## Quick privacy checklist

- [ ] No names, addresses, or phone numbers in AI prompts
- [ ] No vet clinic details in AI prompts
- [ ] No prescription or microchip numbers in AI prompts
- [ ] No client information in AI prompts
- [ ] Real contact info kept on paper only
- [ ] Health decisions confirmed with a veterinarian
