# Printable Care Sheets

Print these one-page sheets and post them where you need them: on the fridge, by the food bowl, in the pet's travel bag, or handed to a pet sitter.

## What's included

| Sheet | What it's for |
|-------|---------------|
| Medication log | Track doses, times, and who gave them |
| Vet visit log | Record visits, prescriptions, and follow-ups |
| Feeding schedule | Meal times, portions, and treats |
| Behavior notes | Observations to share with your vet |
| Pet-sitter care sheet | Everything a sitter needs in one page |
| Emergency contacts | Who to call and what to do |

## How to print

From the static site: open `site/print.html` in your browser and press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P). The print stylesheet hides navigation and prints only the care sheets.

From these docs: print any markdown page, or copy a table into a spreadsheet and print from there.

## Templates (CSV)

Open these in any spreadsheet app (Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice, or a free online equivalent):

- `templates/medication-log.csv`
- `templates/vet-visit-log.csv`
- `templates/feeding-schedule.csv`
- `templates/behavior-log.csv`

Print blank copies and fill them in by hand, or type directly into the spreadsheet.
