# Pet-Sitter Instructions

Leave your pet sitter clear, calm, written instructions — so they feel confident and your pet feels safe.

## Why it matters

Sitters do their best work when they know exactly what to do, when to do it, and who to call. Written instructions reduce phone calls and guesswork for everyone.

## Step 1: Fill out the care sheet template

Use the printable template in `templates/pet-sitter-care-sheet.md` (or `site/print.html`). Include:

- Your pet's name, species, and age
- Where food, treats, leash, and supplies are kept
- Feeding times and amounts
- Medication times and doses (if any)
- Walk or play routines
- Where your pet sleeps
- Favorite hiding spots or things that scare your pet
- House rules (furniture, rooms, doors to keep closed)

## Step 2: Ask AI to write a sitter welcome note

**Example prompt:**

> "Help me write a short, friendly welcome note for a pet sitter covering: feeding twice daily at 8 AM and 6 PM, one 20-minute walk after dinner, medication with breakfast, and where to find supplies. Keep the tone warm and the steps numbered."

Edit it until it sounds like you. Add or remove anything that doesn't fit your home.

## Step 3: Emergency section (see also Emergency Contacts)

Leave a printed emergency page with:

- Your vet's phone number (general label only in digital copies)
- The nearest emergency clinic
- A backup contact who can make decisions if you can't be reached
- Your pet's carrier location and how to get your pet into it

## Step 4: Test it

Before you leave, walk the sitter through the sheet together. Ask them to read it back to you. Fix anything unclear.

## Privacy reminder

Don't put your home address, full name, or client details in a digital copy you share online. Print the sheet and hand it over, or share a copy with names and addresses removed.
