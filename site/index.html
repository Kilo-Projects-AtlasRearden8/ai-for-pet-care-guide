Welcome

Caring for a pet means juggling a lot: medications, vet visits, meals, behavior notes, and plans for when you're away. This manual shows you how to use AI tools — plainly, step by step, privately — to keep it all straight.

1. Getting started

Pick an AI tool, write your first pet-care prompt, and print your first care sheet. No technical experience needed.

2. Track medications

Build reminder schedules, log doses, and set up refill prompts — safely and privately.

3. Track vet visits

Prepare for appointments, organize visit notes, and keep a visit history.

4. Feeding schedules

Create weekly meal charts, track portions and treats, and post them where you feed your pet.

5. Behavior notes

Turn everyday observations into organized notes your vet can use.

6. Pet-sitter instructions

Print-ready care sheets so anyone watching your pet knows exactly what to do.

7. Emergency contacts

One page with who to call, what to bring, and how to get to the clinic fast.

8. Privacy & safety

Never share client or personal data with AI tools. Keep real contact info on paper only.

9. Printable care sheets

One-page sheets to print and post on the fridge, plus blank templates.

Privacy first: Never put your name, address, veterinarian's name, prescription numbers, or client information into a public AI tool. Use nicknames and general descriptions instead. See Privacy & Safety.
