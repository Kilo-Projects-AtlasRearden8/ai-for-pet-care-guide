Tracking vet visits with AI

Turn scattered appointment notes into one clear place — so your next vet visit is productive and nothing falls through the cracks.

Step 1: Prepare before the appointment

"I'm taking my dog to the vet next week. He's been [limping / scratching / drinking more water than usual]. Help me write a short list of questions to ask the vet, and a list of details to bring (food brand, medications, recent changes). Keep it plain and simple."

Print the list or keep it on your phone. Bring it into the exam room.

Step 2: Take notes during or right after the visit

  • Date and clinic name (general only — no need for addresses in AI tools)
  • What the vet examined
  • Diagnoses or suspected issues, in the vet's own words
  • Medications prescribed (name, dose, frequency)
  • Next steps and follow-up date
  • Anything you don't understand — ask the vet to explain before you leave

Step 3: Ask AI to organize your notes

"Here are my notes from a vet visit: [paste your handwritten notes, no names or addresses]. Turn them into a simple summary: what was found, what was prescribed, what to watch for, and when to follow up. Use plain language."

Review the AI summary against your original notes. Correct anything that doesn't match what the vet said.

Safety checks

  • Always confirm diagnoses and doses with your vet — AI can misread handwritten notes
  • Never share your clinic's name, address, or your pet's microchip number in a public AI tool
  • Call the vet if anything in the AI summary contradicts what you were told
