Feeding schedules with AI

Regular meals keep pets healthy — and a written schedule makes it easy for anyone in the household to feed the right amount at the right time.

Step 1: Write down what you already know

  • What food does your pet eat? (brand and type)
  • How much per meal?
  • How many meals per day?
  • What times?
  • Treats? How many, and when?

Step 2: Ask AI to build a weekly feeding chart

"I have a 3-year-old dog who eats twice a day, about 1 cup per meal, at 7 AM and 6 PM. He gets two small treats after his walk. Help me make a simple weekly feeding chart, Monday through Sunday, with meal times and a place to check off each meal. Keep it to one page."
  1. Check portion sizes against the food bag and your vet's advice.
  2. Adjust times to match your household routine.
  3. Print it and post it where you feed your pet.

Step 3: Track treats and changes

Pets often get extra treats from family members. Add a "treats given" line to your chart so nothing is double-counted.

Step 4: When food needs change

If your pet's age, weight, or activity changes, ask your vet before adjusting portions. You can ask AI to draft questions to bring up:

"My dog is getting less exercise this winter. What should I ask my vet about adjusting his food portions?"

Never change food or portions based on an AI suggestion alone — confirm with your vet.

Safety checks

  • Compare chart portions with the food packaging and your vet's guidance
  • Note any food allergies on the chart so anyone feeding your pet sees them
  • Keep fresh water available at all times
