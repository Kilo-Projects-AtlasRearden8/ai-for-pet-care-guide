Printable care sheets

Print these one-page sheets and post them where you need them: on the fridge, by the food bowl, in the pet's travel bag, or handed to a pet sitter.

SheetWhat it's for
Medication logTrack doses, times, and who gave them
Vet visit logRecord visits, prescriptions, and follow-ups
Feeding scheduleMeal times, portions, and treats
Behavior notesObservations to share with your vet
Pet-sitter care sheetEverything a sitter needs in one page
Emergency contactsWho to call and what to do

How to print

Press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P) on this page. Your browser will print only the care sheets — navigation and site chrome are hidden automatically.

Blank CSV templates are in templates/ and open in any spreadsheet app (Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice, or a free online equivalent). Print blank copies and fill them in by hand.
