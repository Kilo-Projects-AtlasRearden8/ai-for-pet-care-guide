# AI for Pet Care — Beginner Manual & Static Site

A plain-English, step-by-step beginner manual for using AI to care for pets: tracking medications, vet visits, feeding schedules, and behavior notes — plus pet-sitter instructions, emergency contacts, printable care sheets, and a privacy-first approach.

Published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — Indiana, formed October 2026. Beginner-friendly AI education, books, and guides. Learn more at dylanasdale.github.io.

## What's inside

- `docs/` — Markdown manual: getting started, medications, vet visits, feeding, behavior notes, pet-sitter instructions, emergency contacts, privacy & safety, printable care sheets
- `site/` — Static HTML site (no build step, no dependencies): open `site/index.html` in any browser
- `templates/` — Blank CSV logs and a pet-sitter care sheet template
- `LICENSE` — MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Asdale Dynamics, LLC

## How to use it

1. Read the privacy section first (`docs/08-privacy-and-safety.md` or `site/08-privacy.html`).
2. Work through the manual one section at a time. Copy the example AI prompts, edit them with general details only, and print the results.
3. Print care sheets from `site/print.html` (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) — the print stylesheet hides navigation and prints only the sheets.
4. Fill in the CSV templates in `templates/` by hand or in any spreadsheet app.

## Privacy (read first)

Never share client or personal data with AI tools: no names, addresses, phone numbers, veterinarian details, prescription numbers, microchip numbers, or client information. Use nicknames and general descriptions. Keep real contact info on paper only.

## Not veterinary advice

This project is educational information only. It is not veterinary advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for your pet's health needs.
